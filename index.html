The short version. Erovoutika Robot Controller does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind. Everything the app remembers is stored on your own phone and stays there.

We have written the long version anyway, because “we don’t collect anything” is a claim that deserves to be checked rather than trusted.

01 Information we collect

None. Erovoutika does not receive any information about you or your device from this app. The app has no server, no account system, no crash reporting, no analytics library and no advertising identifier.

02 Information the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of your own setup so it survives closing the app. This is stored in Android’s private app storage, is readable only by this app, and is never uploaded anywhere:

  • Names you give to your control channels (for example renaming “D3” to “Horn”)
  • How many toggle buttons or sliders each panel shows
  • Slider defaults such as speed, line threshold and obstacle distance
  • Two preferences: whether sound and vibration feedback are on

Device backup and device-to-device transfer are deliberately switched off for this app, so even these settings are not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

 To find your robot’s HC-05, HC-06, HM-10 or ESP32 module and open a serial connection to it Device names and addresses are shown in the picker and held in memory for the length of the session. They are not saved and never leave the phone

ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Android 11 and older only 		Android 11 and below had no separate scanning permission, so the operating system required location permission before any app could scan for Bluetooth or Wi-Fi devices The app has no location feature. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, storage, SMS, phone, calendar or background-location permissions.

04 Voice Control and the microphone

The Voice Control panel does not open the microphone. It hands a request to Android’s own voice-input dialog — the same one used across your phone — and that system component does the listening under its own permission. The app only ever receives the resulting text, uses it to work out a driving command, and discards it.

This means your speech is processed by whichever speech-recognition provider your phone is configured to use (on most devices, Google). That processing is governed by that provider’s privacy policy, not ours, and happens whether or not this app is involved. Erovoutika never receives your voice or the transcript.

05 Internet connections the app makes

The app works fully offline. Every controller, all bundled Arduino sketches, the command wiki, the company page and every bundled image are on the device. Connections are made only when you open a feature that needs one:

  • shop.erovoutika.ph — the in-app shop browser, and product photographs on the promotional strip
  • docs.arduino.cc — the Arduino documentation browser
  • erovoutika-iot.web.app — the IoT demonstration page
  • erovoutika.ph — the company website
  • facebook.com — only if you tap a news item; this opens in the Facebook app or your browser, not inside this app
  • 192.168.4.1 — your own ESP32, over your own local network. This traffic never reaches the internet

These are ordinary web pages. When you visit one, it can set its own cookies and keep its own server logs exactly as it would in any browser, under its own privacy policy. We do not add tracking to them and we do not receive anything about your visit.

06 Advertising

The app shows a slim, dismissible strip promoting Erovoutika’s own products, labelled AD. It is drawn from a product list bundled inside the app.

There is no advertising SDK, no ad network, no ad personalisation, no advertising ID and no measurement of any kind. The strip does not know who you are, does not report that it was shown, and does not report that it was tapped. Nothing about you is used to choose which product appears.

07 Children

This app is not directed to children under 13. It is a technical tool for students, hobbyists and trainees working with Arduino hardware, and its Google Play target audience is set to 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — because we do not collect personal information at all.

08 Sharing and selling

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. No data is sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties, advertisers, data brokers or affiliates. There is no situation in which this can change without a new version of the app and an update to this policy.

09 Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no store of user data to breach. Within the app:

  • All external web traffic uses HTTPS. Plain HTTP is permitted for exactly one address — 192.168.4.1, your own ESP32, which cannot hold a certificate — and is blocked everywhere else
  • Local settings live in Android’s private per-app storage
  • Release builds are code-shrunk and obfuscated, and are signed with a key held only by Erovoutika

10 Your rights

You can erase everything the app has stored by clearing the app’s data in Android Settings → Apps → Erovoutika Robot Controller → Storage, or by uninstalling it. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or delete on our side — but you are welcome to write to us with any question about this policy.
