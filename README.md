# Erovoutika Robot Controller — Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for the **Erovoutika Robot Controller** Android app
(`com.erovoutika.btcontroller`), published at:

**https://engrrynn.github.io/Erovoutika-Robot-Controller/**

That URL is registered with Google Play. **Do not rename or delete this
repository, and do not turn off GitHub Pages.** Google Play requires a live,
publicly reachable privacy policy for every listed app, and a policy URL that
starts returning 404 causes app **suspension** — not merely a rejection at
review time.

## What is here

Exactly one page: `index.html`, a self-contained document with no external
assets beyond a Google Fonts stylesheet. Nothing else belongs in this
repository — that is the point of it existing separately from the app source.

## Editing it

The canonical text lives with the app, in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md`. Edit there
first, then carry the change into `index.html` here, into the bundled copy the
app ships (`lib/constants/privacy_policy.dart`), and into the other renderings
under the app's `docs/`.

Move the effective date whenever the wording changes, and note material changes
in the Play release notes. A policy that disagrees with itself across two URLs
is worse than one that is merely out of date — Play compares them.
